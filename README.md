# Brew or Die

A 2D pixel-art platformer about a witch collecting ingredients to brew potions for her customers.

## About the Game

**Brew or Die** is a 2D platformer developed in Unity

You play as Mavis, a young witch living in a cabin in the woods. She must explore the forest, collect magical ingredients, avoid dangerous creatures and hazards, and prepare the correct potions for her customers.

## Character

**Mavis** is a witch who lives in a small cabin deep in the forest. She collects unusual ingredients and brews potions to fulfill her customers' requests.

## Gameplay

- Explore a forest level.
- Collect ingredients and other collectibles.
- Avoid enemies and environmental hazards.
- Use checkpoints to save your progress.
- Reach the end of the level and complete your potion-brewing adventure.

## Controls

| Key | Action |

## How to Play

1. Start the game from the title screen.
2. Explore the forest and collect ingredients.
3. Avoid enemies and hazards.
4. Reach the cauldron and brew the requested potion.
5. Complete the level to reach the Level Complete screen.

## Customers & Their Potions

Throughout the game, Mavis receives potion requests from three customers, each with their own unusual needs.

### Priscilla – Dramatic Entrance Potion
A spoiled, attention-loving girl who believes every entrance deserves an audience. She wants a potion that guarantees everyone notices her the moment she enters a room.

**Potion:** Dramatic Entrance  
**Description:** A magical concoction that makes the drinker impossible to ignore, ensuring every arrival is accompanied by an unforgettable entrance.

### Barnaby – Sock Summoner Potion
A rather peculiar customer who has apparently had enough of losing his socks. He's looking for a magical solution to his never-ending laundry problem.

**Potion:** Sock Summoner  
**Description:** A potion that allows the drinker to summon socks out of thin air, hopefully putting an end to missing pairs forever.

### Winnie – Fake Productivity Potion
A girl who wants to look busy without actually having to do any work. Efficiency isn't exactly her priority.

**Potion:** Fake Productivity  
**Description:** A potion that creates the illusion of being incredibly productive, making everyone believe the drinker is hard at work even when they're doing absolutely nothing.
